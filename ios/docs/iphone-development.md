# BoringTravel on iPhone: development plan

Written 18 Nov 2026, when this project was started. For Street Gym's plan, which this one follows, see `ios-streetgym/docs/iphone-development.md`; for background on every suite app, the suite's `IOS-PORTING-REPORT.md`.

## Where it stands

- **The engine is shared.** Since 18 Nov 2026 the trip, plan and journal rows, their rules, the trip ideas and the backup format are the module `android-boringtravel/shared`, which the Android app (1.21.1) and this app both run on. What is in it, how it is checked and what is left out: `android-boringtravel/docs/shared-engine.md`.
- **Trips and plans are editable (18 Nov 2026), ported screen by screen from Android:**
  - **The frame:** Android's bottom bar, drawn per theme: Trips · Calendar · Map · Community · Me outside a trip, the trip's own Plan · Journal · Map · Media inside one. The rules are the Android app's `ui/nav/TripNav.kt`, compiled here as it is: one trip on the stack, Back leaves it in one step, a trip opens on Plan. Community is Android's since round 24 (below), a trip's Media since round 20.
  - **Maps (18 Nov 2026)** are Android's: the Map tab (Globe · Map, kept as `mapView`; the trip on now, "All trips" / "Today's trip", every trip's routes, stops and located notes, "Show everything", "No routes yet") and a trip's Map tab (Travelled · Planned, the map's look Street · Terrain · Earth kept for every trip map in Android's own `map_style` form, "Show the whole trip", "18.4 km today · 3 stops", the leg sheet "Day 13 · Leg 9 of 21" with Previous and Next, its title opening the destination's place page). Pins open their place page (a plan place, a stop) or the note. The map is MapLibre Native for iOS 7.0.0, the library Android uses, on OpenFreeMap's tiles (no key): Kotlin can't bind it on Windows, so a short Swift class (`iosApp/BoringTravel/MapLibreMap.swift`) draws what Kotlin hands it through two small interfaces (`iosMain/…/IosMap.kt`: `NativeMaps`, `NativeMap`, `NativeMapListener`): a whole style as JSON, the GeoJSON of the app's six sources, the pins as PNGs, where the camera goes; it reports the style loaded or failed, the camera and taps. Everything else is common Kotlin: OpenFreeMap's positron or dark style fetched once and kept in the caches (`MapBaseStyles`), Terrain's shading and Earth's pictures and the recolouring from the Android app's `clip/MapStyles.kt` (read in), the app's layers on top (`MapLook.kt`), the pins drawn once for both (`MapPictures.kt`), the camera's fit (zoom 15 at most, 1.5 km round one point, room for the chips and the sheet), pins hiding behind each other as MapLibre's icon padding hides them, and what a tap reaches, nearest of notes, stops, plan places, then routes (`MapCamera.kt`, `BtMap.kt`). The desktop draws a stand-in (`MapKit.kt`: the countries' outlines, the same pins and lines, the same camera), so the screens, their tests and the screenshots run on Windows. Opening a map, and starting the app for the trips on now or ended in the last three days, turn the recorded points into stops named by the phone's geocoder and given a country (`TrackProcessor.kt`, Android's rules read in); opening the Plan ticks items the route passed within 200 m on their day (VisitMatcher, read in). This phone records no route yet (round 10), so routes come from Android backups; without one, the stops are joined in time order.
  - **Dictation and check-ins (18 Nov 2026)** are Android's. Dictation: the mic at the end of Write's toolbar and on Trips' Now card (Write, dictating), the first-use word about the microphone and Settings after a refusal, "Listening · Greek" with level bars, words at the cursor corrected live and fixed at each pause, Me › Voice notes (kept as `dictationLanguage`), the same recovery and messages. The rules are the Android app's (`speech/DictationText.kt`, `DictationRecovery.kt`, read in); its Dictation is written again over `SpeechEngine` (`ios/Dictation.kt`), on iPhone the Speech framework with AVAudioEngine (`iosMain/…/IosSpeech.kt`: on the phone when it has the language, else online; punctuation added; an utterance ends after 2.5 s without new words, as Android's recognizer ends one; Apple's errors read as Android's codes). The daily check-in card on a trip's Journal: today's places, "Yes, write" making today's check-in note with its place chip, Another place, Use current location (`PhoneLocation.fix`, Core Location), Skip, asked once per trip and day on this phone (`ios/CheckIns.kt`, with the Android app's `checkin/DailyCheckIn.kt` read in).
  - **Your world (18 Nov 2026)** is Android's. The Map tab opens on the Globe ("Since 2026"): the app's own globe of the countries' outlines, visited ones in the highlighter, planned and want-to-go ones dashed, round flag stickers with leader lines and names where they fit, drag and fling, pinch, double tap home, the idle spin (off with Reduce Motion or a sheet open), a tap on a flag or a country; the sheet below (count, stats, then Your world pulled up: tiles, continents, flags collected, want to go, totals, Share, Places I've been); a country's sheet (its best photo, first trip, trips · days · places, its trips, Show on the map, I've been here, Plan a trip here, Want to go, Remove) and a continent's; Places I've been (every country in the phone's language, search, tap to add, hold to remove after asking); Share your world (the 9:16 card, the share sheet, Save image to Photos).   - **The phone's calendar (18 Nov 2026)** is Android's: the trip's ⋮ › Calendar sheet (what goes in, the question before access, the calendars by account, Add N events, linked with Keep calendar updated, Update and Remove, Share .ics file, Import from calendar, the time zone), the Plan tab's line when the calendar is behind, the item sheet's Add to calendar (the phone's own new-event screen, no access) and "sync calendar".   - **Backup files (18 Nov 2026)** are Android's: Me › Backup & sync › Save a file (the Files app's save sheet takes it where the traveller picks) and Restore a file (the Files app's picker, then the file's date and counts to confirm), progress and Stop, Undo restore. A restore puts the phone back as the file was, like a database (`backup/BackupMerge.kt`, written again; Android's BackupMergeTest runs against it; `backup/BackupSafety.kt` read in), keeps the phone as it was for Undo (none for a phone with nothing of its own), brings the settings back and ends demo mode; the settings this phone hasn't got yet (the trip video's, the book's, the traveller, Wi-Fi only) travel on to its next backup. A file made on Android restores here and the other way round (`ios/Backups.kt`, `BackupScreen.kt`). Since round 23 the same screen backs up to Google Drive (below).

The desktop draws the same HTML with a stand-in of its own (`desktopMain/…/DesktopPrint.kt`) into a real PDF of page pictures, the HTML beside it. `exports/` is emptied when the app starts. Google Docs saves the trip as a Doc in Drive › BoringTravel › Exports since round 23, with Open and Share link.

  - **Route tracking, notifications, widgets (19 Nov 2026)** are Android's. Me › Route tracking (its row with the level, Off until one is picked: nothing is asked at the first start; "Now" with Start recording and Stop for today; the level for every trip; the steps one at a time), a trip map's Route tracking button and sheet (the trip's own level, "Starts on …" before the trip) and the dot on the distance chip while it records. ## Approach

The same as Street Gym's:

- **No Mac.** Code is written on the Windows PC. GitHub's macOS runners compile the app, run it in the iPhone Simulator and return screenshots.
- **One code base for what can be shared.** The Android app is already written in Compose, so its screens can run on iPhone through Compose Multiplatform once they stop calling Android and Java directly. That is the main work.
- **Swift only where Apple requires it or Kotlin can't reach from Windows:** the app entry point and the map's view (MapLibre, an Objective-C framework that only a Mac can bind for Kotlin) now; later StoreKit, the video editor, widgets.
- **The desktop target** runs the same screens in a window on Windows and writes a PNG of each, so most checks need no CI round trip.

As on Android today (its background recording is off too), the route stops when the app is closed or iOS ends it, and a quiet note says so when the nightly check next runs (Android: the same note after a restart). Each fix waits in a small inbox in the settings and goes into the trip store in batches (the store is still one text: rewriting it for every fix would be slow).
- **Widgets:** iPhone has no one-row widget: the Quick bar is the medium widget with the bar's row across the theme's paper (its stamp, Voice and text step aside by Android's widths); the small widgets' two buttons each open their own action on iOS 17 (App Intents), the whole widget opens Write on iOS 16. The widgets keep the system font, as Android's. They need the owner's App Group to show anything on a real iPhone.
- **Trip video:** the picture, its preview, the editor, the export and sharing (rounds 18 to 20). The export pauses while the app is away (an iPhone's encoder stops then) and goes on when it is back, in a new part from the next frame; iOS may end an app left in the background, and then the export is lost (Android's foreground service goes on). Watch is the phone's own full-screen player (Android draws its own controls). TikTok, Instagram and WhatsApp take the video from the share sheet, through their own share extensions (Android hands it to the app straight; TikTok's own posting needs its Share Kit and the owner's TikTok developer app). Save to phone puts it in Photos ("Saved to Photos."; Android: Movies › BoringTravel). Drive says it isn't connected (round 23). A restored clip has no picture in the Media tab until it is made again. The swipe from the left edge is Android's Back in the editor (a panel closes, then the scene goes), as are a panel's Done and the bar's Let go; the full-screen preview closes on it as Android's dialog does. A phone video's own sound and the voice lines are heard only in the export (as on Android); the preview's video pictures are taken by AVAssetImageGenerator within a sixteenth of a second of each time asked (Android decodes every picture). The studio's status bar is light, as Android's studio sets it. The full-screen preview covers the screen (Android: a dialog), closed by its Close or Back. The route is the first the trip can make (Trip plan, else Recorded journey, else Saved stops); choosing another is the Route tool's (round 20). Me › Your traveller & rides isn't here (round 20): a restored backup's traveller and rides walk and ride the video.
- **Book:** the overview, the Book look, Add a page, Photo pages, the page editor with every tool and the layers list, and the PDF (rounds 21, 22). The swipe from the left edge is Android's Back in the page editor (the tool, the selection, all pages), as are the panel's Done, a tap beside the page and All pages. On the desktop a dragged element moves on the page once the finger lifts (its stand-in has no JavaScript); on iPhone it follows the finger as on Android. Send to Drive saves the PDF in Drive › BoringTravel › Exports (round 23). Save to phone goes through the Files app's save sheet (Android: its "create document" picker); Print hands the PDF to the iPhone's print dialog on the printer's paper nearest the book's sheet (Android: a print job at the book's media size). A sheet's foot has a few tenths of a millimetre of the book's paper colour where WebKit's printed page ends (each section is a pixel shorter than WebKit's page, so it never runs onto the next sheet). "Too many large photos for one go…" can't show: an iPhone ends an app out of memory. The page on screen and the PDF are WebKit's on iPhone (Android's are Chromium's), with the gradients in their WebKit forms.
- **Back** is the top bar's button and, since the parity round, the swipe from the left edge (Android's system Back); at Trips it does nothing, where Android's leaves the app.
- **Date texts and numbers** are in the phone's language as on Android (since the parity round). The plan's clock is 24-hour on both.
