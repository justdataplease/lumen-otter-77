# BoringTravel for iPhone

The iPhone app, started on 18 Nov 2026. It runs on the same engine as the Android app (`android-boringtravel/shared`: the trips, the plan's rules, the trip ideas, the backup format), and compiles the Android app's own themes, fonts, icons and components, read from `android-boringtravel/` at build time.

**Where it stands:** everything is kept between starts (the trips in a file of their own beside the photos since round 28), in all five themes. Android's toasts show as the app's own pill near the foot of the screen (iOS has none). The screens, each ported from Android's:

- **The bar:** Trips · Calendar · Map · Community · Me, and inside a trip its Plan · Journal · Map · Media, drawn per theme.
- **Community:** Android's own screens: signed out, the sign-in card (Google, and Sign in with Apple beside it) with a quiet sign-in when the phone's account is there; Latest, Following and Top (this month, all time); a post's page (its pictures, the heart, the trip's plan on a small map with its days and notes, an album, a clip played full screen, Share, Copy this trip into your trips with or without dates); profiles and their follow lists, Follow, Community profile with the Blocked list; Report (a post, a profile) and Block, and "Something wrong? Write to …" (Apple 1.2); posting (a trip's ⋮ › Post to community, Community's +): a trip's plan with the notes picked, a saved clip or an album of photos, the cover, title and caption, the preview, the community rules the first time, the photos and clip up to a link-shared BoringTravel/Community folder in the poster's own Drive (1600 px, without their location), Try again and Stop; the trip's ⋮ › Community post to update the post by hand; Delete post; Me's Community profile. Offline it shows what was saved and keeps likes, reports, blocks and follows until it is back. A post's link opens it (`https://justdataplease.com/apps/boringtravel/post/#…` once the site has the owner's file, `boringtravel://post/#…` now); New trip's "Start from a community trip". Firestore and Firebase Auth through their REST APIs, the posters' pictures and clips from their Drive by link. It needs the owner's Firebase ids and Drive API key; without them the tab says "Not in this build". The tests and pictures run on a fake Firebase and Drive.
- **Travel together:** a trip's ⋮ › Travel together (and the header's "+N" badge): invite friends with one link, see who is in and when the trip last synced, Sync now, Leave trip; Trips' "Join a friend's trip" and join links opened with the app open the Join sheet. Each member's copy sits in their own Google Drive, link-shared, read by the others with the app's Drive API key and merged (the later edit wins, deletions travel); the trip's mailbox in Firestore tells the members where each copy is. Every change goes about 4 s later, waits for a connection and is kept through closing the app; a trip on screen syncs every 10 s, the others every 3 h in the background (best effort). Android's own sheets and rules; it needs the owner's Google iPhone client, Firebase ids and Drive API key, and runs on fakes in the tests and pictures.
- **Sharing into the app:** BoringTravel in other apps' share sheets for text, a link, 1–10 pictures and a text file (a Share Extension, `iosApp/BoringTravelShare`). Apple gives a share extension no way to open its app, so it keeps what was shared in the App Group's folder and says "Sent to BoringTravel. Open the app to finish."; the next time the app is on screen it opens Android's own sheets, by Android's own rules: Add to plan for a link (the link's preview, the trip on now and today first, Place / Food / Stay, Add, then the Plan on that day; "Copy its notes and pictures" for a blog or a Google Doc; a Google Maps list's "Add its N places"), the planner for longer text, a Google Doc's link, a .txt file ("Reading the document") and pictures, the Join sheet for a trip's link, "Nothing to add: share a link" for a few words. What waits for Welcome or survives iOS closing the app is kept with the screens. It needs the owner's App Group for the extension's App ID.
- **Welcome:** the first start, shown once, with Android's account step: Continue with Google (the account and Drive in one go), Sign in with Apple beside it, Use without an account, then a backup in Drive found and restored.
- **Account and Google Drive:** Me's Account card (Google or Apple, Drive's state, Sign out (of the community too), Delete account: the community's profile, posts, likes and follows first, Apple's token revoked, the own AI keys wiped, Drive's access given back) and Me › Backup & sync with Google Drive: Connect, Disconnect, Back up now with its steps, the backups in Drive to restore (with Undo), Back up every day around 22:00 and Only on Wi‑Fi, the newest 10 kept, every photo and saved video once in Drive; the restore offer on a new phone and Trips' line when backups stall. Google's sign-in page and Apple's sheet on iPhone, Drive's REST API over Ktor, the daily backup through BGTaskScheduler. They need the owner's Google iPhone client (and for Apple the App ID's capability and Firebase's Apple provider); until then the app says "Google sign-in isn't set up in this build." The tests and pictures run on a fake Google.
- **Trips:** the traveller's own trips (now, next, someday, past) as tickets with their countdowns in each trip's colour and their countries' flags, or as pictures (Me › Trips look); the trip on now with Write today, the mic (Write, dictating) and the camera; New trip, Notes outside trips, the trip ideas with their drawn pictures. In demo mode, the example trips instead.
- **New trip / Edit trip:** Make it myself, then the AI ways, a ready-made trip or a community trip; the cover photo (or one of the trip's own), name, route, dates, Photos in notes (the size and frame new photos take); Delete.
- **Trip ideas:** the destinations, then pace and focus over the program, and the first day.
- **A trip's Plan:** the List, collapsed (one line per item) or expanded (every day in full, with the travel between places and a rough time), or the Calendar; today first, ticks, a day's own name or its place, Ideas; the circles show a link's picture, its site's icon or the place's Wikipedia photo; press and hold to drag an item within its day or to another (with Undo), or for Copy name, Copy address and Share; a leg's travel (vehicle, Count in km); Chat (the AI planner) beside Add, as Android 1.22.0; Add from a link in the trip's ⋮; items the recorded route passed ticked.
- **The item sheet:** kind, title, day, time and end (the phone's 12- or 24-hour clock), the place (map search, a name of one's own, a pasted Google Maps link, Use my location), the website with its preview (Paste a link), Getting there, note, tags, Open in Google Maps, its Journal (the notes about it, and Write: a note placed at it), Renew details, Share, Delete.
- **A place page:** a day's title or a pin on the map opens it: the theme's hero, the country, how long, the plan's days, Getting there, Where to stay, Notes, In your plan, Add a place; a stop's Add to plan and Remove.
- **Copy from a link:** a blog's posts (Tumblr, WordPress, Blogger, Substack, Medium and other sites, a feed, a site's dated articles, one post's link) or a Google Doc become notes with their words and pictures as they are, sorted into trips by the AI (or by their dates), their places pinned and put in the plan; a shared Google Maps list's places become a trip's ideas. Into new trips or one you have; it goes on when its screen is left, and asks the phone for time in the background. Me › Import trips from a link, a trip's Add from a link, the planner's offer.
- **A trip's Media:** its saved trip videos (played in Android's own player, shared, deleted), then its photos and videos by day (a tap opens the note, press and hold deletes one, Add media).
- **Journal:** each trip's notes (rows with their photo frame and counts), with the daily check-in on top (today's place, Another place, Use current location, Skip), and Write; Notes outside trips (no Write there, as on Android).
- **A note:** every block Android writes (formatting, headings, lists, checklists to tick, quotes, photos and their layouts and frames, videos, places, lines); videos played full screen in Android's own player (a photo is only looked at, as on Android); a place opens Google Maps; Delete.
- **Write:** Android's editor: title, rich text (bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, larger, smaller, highlight, colours, headings, bullets, numbers), quotes, checklists, places, lines, page breaks; the note's font; dictation (the mic: words at the cursor as you speak, in the language of Me › Voice notes); date, time and place (typed, on the map, from the trip, or Use my location); photos and videos from the library or the camera, single or in layouts (frames, captions, angle, size, swap, move, remove), photos in their own format and videos made 720p MP4s as on Android ("Preparing the video… 40%"). Saves as you go; notes written on Android stay intact.
- **Calendar:** every trip by month or year with the flags of where it goes, the week starting where the phone's region says; a day opens the trip there or starts a new one.
- **Me:** theme, fonts, Voice notes, Route tracking, AI, Backup & sync (save a backup file and restore one, Android's format both ways with the trip videos' files, Undo restore), Import trips from a link, Your traveller & rides (who walks and how each vehicle looks in every trip video), Trips look, Plan view, Place photos, Demo mode (Android's example trips: Argentina on now with its real photos, Kyoto next, Lisbon past), privacy policy, version. The settings are kept under the names a backup gives them.
- **Route tracking:** Me › Route tracking (what records now with Start recording and Stop for today, the level for every trip, the steps, as Android's: precise location While Using, then notifications) and a trip map's own level, with a dot on its distance chip while it records. It records during the traveller's own trip in the background (Core Location, the blue location sign, as Android's location service with its notification; an Always path that keeps it going after the app is closed is built and off, as Android's background recording, one constant away: `IosTrackingPlan.ASK_ALWAYS`), at each level's spacing, Balanced's best fix and Detailed's rest as on Android; the points are the backup's rows, so they move between the phones. A nightly check (BGTaskScheduler) and every start turn points into stops and start or stop the day's recording; quiet notes say when the route stopped or location is off; while it records, a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island says so, with Stop (iOS 17). A note's place falls back on the last route point.
- **Widgets and quick actions:** "Today" (small and medium), "Quick bar" and "Quick bar, small" on the Home Screen (a WidgetKit extension drawing what the app writes for it, in the theme's colours and paper, written again a moment after any change and read every hour; Android's preview in the widget gallery), and the app icon's quick actions Write, Take a photo, Voice note and New trip. They need the owner's App Group on a real iPhone.
- **Your world:** the Map tab's Globe (visited countries filled, planned and wanted ones dashed, flag pins, spin and zoom), Your world's sheet, a country's and a continent's sheet, Places I've been, Share your world (the card to the share sheet or Photos).
- **The phone's calendar:** a trip's ⋮ › Calendar (the trip and its plan into a calendar picked on the phone, kept updated, removed; an .ics file; events imported into the plan), the Plan tab's line when the calendar is behind, an item's Add to calendar. EventKit on iPhone.
- **Trip video:** a trip's ⋮ › Make a clip and the trip map's Play the route clip: the dark studio with the trip's name, the size (9:16, 4:5, 1:1, 16:9) and the preview of the clip, every frame drawn by the Android app's own renderer (the globe from space with the trip's flags, the route drawn with the paper vehicle or the walking traveller at its head, the place names, the photos, day cards, the stats, the end card with the mark), played, paused and scrubbed full screen. The editor under it is Android's: the time row with Undo and Redo, the five tracks (scenes, photos, texts, music, voice) under a fixed playhead to scrub and pinch, a scene chosen on them and its name or card dragged on the picture, Skip stop, and the Text, Audio, Media and Camera tools for the whole clip or one scene (every text, day cards, tags; the eight bundled tracks listened to, where the music starts, on the beat, the music under the preview in step, a voice line a place recorded; the photos per place in their order, the phone's videos from the library or the journal copied in, trimmed and moved; the map, flat or 3D, how close, close-ups), and Length, Route (with the video's own traveller and rides), Look and Stats. - **Share:** a trip's ⋮ › Share: the real pages previewed (Notebook or Minimal, the days, photo size, paper, the plan), then a PDF to save, print or share, the trip as text (save, copy, share) or a Word file. - **The book:** a trip's ⋮ › Make a book (or Share's "Make a book"): the trip laid out as a printed book in the dark studio and kept, every page as a thumbnail (tap to open it in the page editor; hold and drag to move it), Add a page (the 16 layouts), Photo pages (photos picked in order, an album layout, From phone), the Book look (Notebook, Scrapbook, Postcard, Minimal; eight font sets; A5, 21 × 21 cm, A4, US Letter; dotted, ruled or plain paper; bleed and crop marks; pages in fours; Start the layout again), Undo and Redo, and the PDF at the book's own size to save, share or print. The book's code is Android's own, its text measured with Core Text on the fonts the pages print in, its pages WebKit's; a backup carries it to either phone. **The page editor** is Android's own: the page large, exactly as it prints, with "On this page" (a row per element, its eye and handle, the background last) and the tool row; tap to select (outline, corners, the turn handle), drag to move with snapping guides, a corner to resize, the handle to turn in eighths, the page following the finger through WebKit's JavaScript; the tools Layout, Paper, Look, Page (duplicate, use the layout on other pages, add pages, back to the layout, hide the entry, delete), Add (text, photo, tape, corners, stamp, flag, routes, box, circle, line), a photo's Replace, Crop, Frame and Caption, words' Edit, Page text, Font, Colour, Size and Align, Style, Rotate and Arrange, "+ Photos"; every change one step of Undo (30), kept as Android keeps it.

## On Windows

JDK 21. From the suite, through the Gradle gate:

```powershell
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File C:\MyDrive\jdp\android-apps\tools\gradle-gate.ps1 `
  -Root C:\MyDrive\jdp\android-apps\ios-boringtravel -Tasks :composeApp:compileCommonMainKotlinMetadata,:shared:compileIosMainKotlinMetadata,:composeApp:compileIosMainKotlinMetadata,:composeApp:desktopTest,:composeApp:screenshots
```

- `:composeApp:run` opens the screens in a window at iPhone size; the trips made there are kept in `composeApp/build/preview/trips.json`, the settings in `settings.properties` and the notes' photos in `media/` beside it. Choosing photos opens a file dialog; there is no camera and no video player on the desktop (a video shows its frame).
- `:composeApp:screenshots` writes a PNG of every screen to `composeApp/build/screenshots`.
- `:composeApp:compileCommonMainKotlinMetadata` proves the shared code uses no Java or Android API. Run it after changing any Android file on the shared list: the desktop build alone lets Java calls through.
- `:shared:compileIosMainKotlinMetadata` and `:composeApp:compileIosMainKotlinMetadata` type-check the iPhone-only Kotlin (`src/iosMain`) against Apple's frameworks. They don't link or run it: only a Mac does.
- With `-Pkotlin.native.enableKlibsCrossCompilation=true` (Kotlin's klib cross-compilation, since 19 Nov 2026), `:composeApp:compileKotlinIosSimulatorArm64` and `:composeApp:compileTestKotlinIosSimulatorArm64` compile the whole app and the shared tests for the Simulator on Windows, as the Mac's first step does (no linking, no Objective-C header: the Mac still does those). It isn't on by default, so the Linux job stays as it is.
- `src/commonTest` runs in `:composeApp:desktopTest` here and on the Simulator on the Mac (`:composeApp:iosSimulatorArm64Test`): keep it to kotlin.test and the coroutines' test runner (a desktop test checks it names nothing of Java).
- Build values, in the git-ignored `local.properties` or the environment, never in git: `firebase.projectId`, `firebase.appId`, `firebase.apiKey`, `firebase.aiModel` (`BORINGTRAVEL_FIREBASE_*`) for the built-in planner and Sign in with Apple's account; `google.iosClientId` (`BORINGTRAVEL_GOOGLE_IOS_CLIENT_ID`), the Google iPhone OAuth client for sign-in and Drive; `apple.signIn=true` (`BORINGTRAVEL_APPLE_SIGN_IN`) to offer Sign in with Apple. Without them the app says Android's "isn't set up in this build".

## Changing shared code

- **The engine** (`android-boringtravel/shared`): its rules and checklist are in `android-boringtravel/docs/shared-engine.md`.
- **The screen files in `sharedFiles`** (`composeApp/build.gradle.kts`) are compiled for both phones. Keep them free of Android and Java imports, and run the checks above. A few lines are rewritten on the way in (`rewrites`); the build stops if such a line changes.
